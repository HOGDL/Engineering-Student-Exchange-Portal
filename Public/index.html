Welcome to Menofia Engineering Equipment Exchange

A non-profit platform designed for Faculty of Electronic Engineering students to exchange, donate, or sell used lab tools, drawing instruments, and reference textbooks at low prices.

How It Works

  • Browse: Check available items listed by senior students.
  • Offer: List your unused tools and books for incoming students.
  • Request: Reserve or request any available item directly.
